Dorowet

Description

Dorowet is an ETHIOPIAN cultural food and it is made on special holodays. actially it is a litle hard to make but don't worry we will make it simple as much as possible

Ingredients

  • chicken
  • onion
  • oil
  • berbere (pepper)
  • spices
  • cooked egg(boiked egg)

Steps

  1. first we made the sauce
  2. cook the onion
  3. add the oil
  4. mix well and cook them togerther
  5. add berbere(the pepper)
  6. mix well and cook them togerther with water as a cooker
  7. add spices
  8. and now let's readythe chiken
  9. boil water
  10. slay the chicken
  11. separet it well and wash
  12. add the chicken to the sauce and mix
  13. mix well and cook them togerther with water as a cooker
  14. add water
  15. add spices
  16. add the egg
  17. well done
