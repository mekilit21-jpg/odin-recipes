pasta

Description

This upside-down banana bread pops out of the pan with a caramelized banana top. It makes a rich, delicious side dish or even a light dessert. Also a great use for overripe bananas.

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 1/4 cups lightly packed light brown sugar, divided
  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, softened, divided
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups mashed overripe bananas
  • 1 small banana, halved lengthwise and crosswise, or more as needed

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (180 degrees C). Coat a 9x5-inch loaf pan with cooking spray; set aside.
  2. Combine flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl; set aside. Combine 3/4 cup brown sugar and 1/2 cup butter in a large bowl; beat with an electric mixer on medium-high speed until lighter in color, scraping down sides of bowl as needed, about 2 minutes. Reduce speed to medium-low and add eggs, 1 at a time, beating well after each addition. Add vanilla and beat until incorporated. Using a spatula, fold in mashed bananas until just combined. Fold in half of the flour mixture until just combined. Repeat with remaining flour mixture. Set batter aside.
  3. Melt remaining 1/4 cup butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Once bubbling, add remaining 1/2 cup brown sugar and let simmer, stirring occasionally, until sugar has dissolved and mixture bubbles and thickens, about 2 minutes. Pour sugar mixture into prepared pan. Arrange banana pieces on top of sugar mixture side by side, cut side down. Pour batter over bananas and spread evenly.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until a wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean, about 1 hour. Let cool in the pan on a wire rack for 5 minutes. Invert cake onto plate and cool at least 20 minutes before serving.
